## When to send

**Group A — Saturday 2026-08-29, 10:00–11:00 JST.** Do not wait until Tuesday. kentaro used
the app today; every day you wait, his memory of whether he pressed 挿入 gets worse. These
are personal Gmail addresses, and a short personal note on a Saturday morning reads as
genuine rather than as a campaign — which is exactly the tone you want. Saturday morning is
fine here precisely *because* it isn't a marketing send window.

**Group B — Monday 2026-08-31, 20:00–21:00 JST.** They have already forgotten the app, so
recency buys you nothing, and Monday evening is when personal inboxes actually get read in
Japan. Exception: `daisuke.tokuyama@andco.group` is a company domain — send that one Monday
**morning, 09:00–10:00**.

**Group C — Monday 2026-08-31, same evening slot.** Not urgent.

**Avoid Sunday evening entirely** — it gets buried under Monday morning's inbox.

Send them one at a time from your own address, not as a bulk send. Eighteen individual
emails is an hour of work and roughly triples the reply rate versus anything that looks
automated.

---

## Two rules before you send anything

1. **Do not cite their usage.** Not the content of their messages, and not the counts
   either. "You used it 7 times and never pressed insert" is accurate and it reads like
   surveillance. Frame every question as *you chasing a bug in your own app*, not as you
   watching them. Same answer comes back, none of the discomfort.
2. **Ask exactly one question, answerable with one character.** Every extra question halves
   the reply rate.
